The two clearing times come from the protective device's time-current curve at the full and at the reduced arcing current; the reduced current can land on a much slower part of the curve, which is why it is checked too. Results are for study and training. A labelled study needs the full system model, device settings and a qualified engineer.

Working

Method: IEEE 1584-2018. Arcing current from Eq. 1 at 600 V, 2.7 kV and 14.3 kV (interpolated above 600 V, Eq. 25 below), enclosure size correction from Eqs. 11–15, incident energy from Eqs. 3–6 and the arc-flash boundary at 1.2 cal/cm² from Eqs. 7–10. The reduced case uses the arcing current variation factor (Eq. 2). Clothing levels follow the NFPA 70E incident energy method. JavaScript port of the MIT-licensed arcflash library, checked against the standard's Annex D worked examples.
